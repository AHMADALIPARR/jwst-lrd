# Continuum-model comparison for the Abell 2744-QSO1 redshift

A controlled, pre-registered comparison of continuum models (and red-edge treatments) for the Hβ + Hα redshift fit of the
QSO1 NIRSpec PRISM x1d spectrum. Tools: `mawk` (data prep / mask / export), GNU Octave (fitting engine; **not** MATLAB),
`gfortran -std=f2018` (independent polynomial cross-check). No Python/JS/TS. Licence: AGPL-3.0-only.

## Headline (read this first)
- **At the pre-registered baseline (D0, 12-pixel Hα window) no tested continuum model is within 3σ of the published
  z = 7.0451.** The highest D0 value is SPL_1C, z = 7.042329 (σ_J = 0.000417), 6.65σ below 7.0451; the BIC-selected model
  SPL_2C gives z = 7.040158 ± 0.000497. The published value lies outside the model range R1 (20 models, D0) =
  [7.038408, 7.042329], the admissible set R2, and the full range R3 over models × scenarios = [7.035841, 7.044288].
- **Red-edge pixels can shift z by about the size of the gap.** Gap to the Fortran centroid 7.04221: Δz = 0.00289 (≈107.7 km/s).
  Hα leave-one-out jackknife SD is 0.00083–0.0035 (3.1–11.9σ_J); pixels 415/418/420 dominate. The SYNTHETIC edge-deficit control
  (one deficient pixel-420 flux, not data) moves z by −3.0e-3 … +2.06e-3 depending on the continuum model.
  Caveat to the headline above: under the red-edge trimming scenarios (drop the last 3–4 pixels, or flux-error masks) **27 of 180
  fits do come within 3σ of 7.0451 *using their own, inflated Δχ²=1 errors*** (closest: PL_1C at D4, z = 7.044288 ± 0.000876, 0.93σ;
  the next four are D4 1C fits at 1.4–1.8σ). Relative to the *median baseline* σ_J = 0.000275 the nearest is 3.0σ away
  (`cc_report` §D). This is the red-edge sensitivity itself, not a result in favour of 7.0451.
- **A 3σ agreement for one fit is NOT significance.** It is one cell out of 180 model×scenario fits chosen after the fact, with the
  BIC selection not persistent under a one-pixel drop (D0 → SPL_2C, D1 → P0_2C), formal errors that are too small for flexible models
  (synthetic noisy coverage 14/30–24/30 for a nominal 68%), and calibration systematics that the pipeline cannot test (red-edge
  throughput, wavelength calibration/LSF, pixel covariance; see `RESULTS.md` "Not tested"). Model-selection freedom plus
  calibration systematics are not in any per-fit σ.
- **No physical cause is claimed.** PASS counts validate arithmetic, not an explanation.

## Fitting configurations (details and rationale in `PREREG.md`)
- Data: 429 pixels, 408 valid (21 masked: non-finite or flux_error ≤ 0); vacuum rest wavelengths Hβ 4862.691 Å, Hα 6564.632 Å (NIST ASD).
- Line template: unit-area Gaussian integrated over each pixel; σ² = σ_LSF² + (σ_int·λ/c)², LSF fixed at 2.2 px FWHM; amplitudes ≥ 0;
  σ_int **profiled** (never fixed) on a grid; one shared z for Hβ and Hα (per-line z also reported); z grid 7.020–7.065, step 1e-5, 3-point parabola refinement.
- Windows: Hβ centre−12…centre+6 pixels; Hα centre−BW … last valid pixel (BW = 12 primary; 8 and 6 as sensitivity).
- Continuum models (10): RM6/RM12/RM20 (3σ-clipped running median, subtracted), P0–P3 (polynomial per window), PL (power law per window),
  PLLIN (shared-index power law + local linear tilt), SPL (natural cubic regression spline). Each with 1C (single Gaussians) and 2C (narrow + broad Hα) = 20 models.
- Scenarios (identical pixel list for all models): D0 baseline, D1–D4 (drop the last 1–4 valid pixels), ERRALL, ERREDGE (flux-error masks); BW8/BW6 sensitivity. 20 × 9 = 180 fit records.
- Model selection: AIC/BIC/χ²_red per model (n = 35 points at D0), selection by lowest BIC with the ΔBIC thresholds of PREREG §4; QBIC and k_cont=0 BIC are exploratory.

## Uncertainty estimates
- **Δχ²=1 profile errors** (σ_int, spectral index, continuum parameters all profiled) — `hwJ` per fit; also χ²_red-rescaled (`hwJscaled`).
  Typical baseline σ_J ≈ 0.00023–0.0005 in z; formal errors are too small for flexible models (see below).
- **Jackknife (Hα leave-one-out, D0)** — each of the 16 Hα-window pixels removed in turn; SD 0.00083–0.0035 in z (3.1–11.9σ_J). Neighbouring pixels are correlated, so this is a spread indicator only.
- **Bootstrap:** *no bootstrap of the real data was run.* The closest item is a parametric Monte-Carlo on **SYNTHETIC** spectra (noisy injection–recovery at z_true = 7.0451, N = 30 per model, fixed seed):

  | model (1C) | mean bias | SD | mean Δχ²=1 half-width | 1σ coverage |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | P1 | +3.8e-5 | 3.3e-4 | 2.8e-4 | 14/30 |
  | P2 | +7.1e-5 | 4.1e-4 | 3.4e-4 | 19/30 |
  | RM12 | +2.9e-5 | 2.3e-4 | 2.8e-4 | 23/30 |
  | SPL | +6.1e-6 | 3.7e-4 | 4.5e-4 | 24/30 |

  Interpretation: the quoted Δχ²=1 errors can be too small for flexible models. N = 30 is small.

## Sensitivity results (all from `results/run.log` §9 / `RESULTS.md`)
- **Selection:** at D0 BIC selects SPL_2C (ΔBIC = 13.4 to P2_2C; k = 15 for n = 35 points — close to over-parameterised); at D1 it selects P0_2C → **not persistent**.
- **Stability** (|z(D1) − z(D0)| < σ_J(D0)): the selected SPL_2C passes (+0.19σ) but only 4 of 20 models do; D2–D4 shifts of SPL_2C are +1.85σ, −2.26σ, −2.57σ.
- **Pixel influence:** pixel 415 is the largest-|Δz| pixel in 9/20 fits, 418 in 7, 420 in 4. The "+1 pixel" side of the ±1 test is untestable (pixels 421–429 are NaN).
- **Grid limits:** of 90 2C records the broad σ is on the lower grid bound in 56 and the upper in 1; narrow σ on 0 in 11 and on 600 in 16 → 2C fits are weakly constrained.
- **SYNTHETIC controls (not data):** noise-free recovery of z_true = 7.0451 for model families containing the generator: |bias| ≤ 2.3e-6 (engine consistent); pixel-420 edge deficit shifts z by −3.0e-3 (RM6) … +2.06e-3 (SPL_1C).
- **Engine cross-checks:** Octave matches the awk-sp numbers (RM12 7.040280, P1 7.041836, P2 7.042200), `awk/continuum.awk` (5e-11), and the independent Fortran fit (P0–P3 at D0, D1 asserted within 2e-5).
- **Not tested:** red-edge flux calibration, wavelength-dependent LSF, wavelength-calibration version, pixel covariance, error floor at the edge, other pipeline/CRDS versions, air-vs-vacuum (an air value would shift z by +2.2e-3). See `RESULTS.md`.

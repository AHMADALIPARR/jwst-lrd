## Juodžbalis et al. 2026 (dynamical mass benchmark)
- Juodžbalis, I., Marconcini, C., D’Eugenio, F., et al. 2026, Nature, 653, 1017–1021.
- Title: A direct black-hole mass measurement in a little red dot at high redshift.
- DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10579-4
- arXiv: 2508.21748 (v2, 1 Sep 2025)
- Received: 30 August 2025; Accepted: 21 April 2026; Published online: 27 May 2026.
- Key results (from abstract and text):
  - Strongly lensed LRD Abell 2744-QSO1 at z = 7.04.
  - Rotation curve inconsistent with nuclear star cluster; well fit by Keplerian rotation around a point mass of ~50 million solar masses.
  - log(M_BH/M_⊙) = 6.75 ± 0.15 (lower limit from the method; inclination unconstrained in the simple fit; full MOKA3D analysis ~7.7 ± 0.3 in related notes).
  - Conservatively M_BH / M_* > 2.
  - Near-pristine environment (metallicity ≲ 0.5 % solar).
  - Indicates a massive black-hole seed in its earliest accretion phase.
- PDF retrieved and stored locally during integration update (sha not committed; available on request or re-download from DOI/arXiv).
